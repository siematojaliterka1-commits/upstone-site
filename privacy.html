Privacy Policy

In force from 22 November 2026.

In short

  • Everything you enter in Upstone stays on your phone.
  • There are no accounts. We have no server holding your data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • When you delete the app, your data goes with it.

1. Who is responsible for the app

The controller is Norbert Krok, ul. Leszczyńskiego 4/29, 50-078 Wrocław, Poland, tax ID (NIP) PL8522700858. For privacy questions write to this address.

2. What stays on your phone

You save this data yourself and only you can reach it. It does not go to us or to anyone else:

  • meals, calories and scanned products,
  • weight, height, age and sex, if you give them,
  • mood, notes, journal and the evening summary,
  • workouts, sets, runs and your own activities,
  • meditation minutes, learning progress and your day streak,
  • your first name, if you type it, and the app settings.

Some of this is health data. That is the reason we do not collect it on a server. We do not see it and we do not process it.

If you back up your phone to iCloud, the app's data may be part of that backup. This happens in your Apple account, under Apple's rules.

3. What leaves the phone, and when

Barcode scanner

When you scan a product or type its code, the app sends that number and the app language to the Open Food Facts database to find the product. Open Food Facts then sees your IP address, as any website does. We send nothing about you. Its rules are in its privacy policy.

Camera

The camera is used only to read a barcode. The app does not save or send pictures.

Subscription

Apple handles the payment. We do not see your card, your name or the email of your Apple account. The app learns from Apple only whether the subscription is active and until when. Apple gives us summary sales reports with no personal details.

Bookshop links

Books have a "Buy" button. It is an affiliate link to Amazon: if you buy something, we may earn a commission. Tapping it takes you out of the app and that shop's rules apply. We pass none of your data to it.

Apple reports

If you agreed in your iPhone settings to share data with app developers, Apple may show us summary statistics and crash reports. They do not let us tell who you are.

A message to us

When you write to us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you (legal basis: our legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We delete them no later than 12 months after the matter is closed.

4. What we do not do

  • We show no ads.
  • We use no third-party tracking or analytics tools.
  • We build no profiles and make no automated decisions.
  • We do not sell or share data.

5. How to delete your data

Delete the app from your phone. All data saved in it goes with it. You can remove the iCloud backup in your Apple account settings. You cancel the subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

6. Your rights

You may ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its use, and complain to the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland) or to the authority in your country. In practice we hold only what you send us by email.

7. Children

The app is not meant for people under 16.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, the new version appears on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts sending your data anywhere, we will ask for your consent in the app first.
